NiYien 移动端隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 15 日

适用范围与联系

本政策适用于包名为 com.niyien.stabilizer 的 NiYien iOS 和 Android 应用。NiYien 运营本应用及其支持服务。技术支持、隐私咨询、查阅、更正或删除请求，请联系 support@niyien.com；无需安装应用或附带诊断资料即可联系。

视频在本地处理

您选择的视频和陀螺仪文件在设备本地处理，用于导入、预览、稳定处理和导出。NiYien 不向自身服务器发送您的视频或音频文件。您主动通过其他服务分享、存储文件，或开启系统备份时，相关服务依照自身条款及您的设备设置处理数据。

关闭自动使用统计

iOS 和 Android 版本不生成或发送匿名安装分析标识、相机使用事件或下载统计，也没有可重新启用这些事件的开关。应用不将这些数据用于广告或跨应用追踪。下文说明的必要网络服务和自愿反馈与使用统计分别处理。

自愿反馈与诊断资料

点击“提交”会发送您填写的问题描述和可选邮箱，并自动附带当前可用的诊断日志、崩溃记录、应用版本、提交时间，以及操作系统、CPU、GPU、内存等技术设备信息。可用的镜头、队列和应用设置也会附带。资料可能含文件名、路径、相机信息、编辑参数、耗时或性能信息，不附加视频或音频媒体。提交是自愿的；您也可直接发邮件联系支持，不附加诊断资料。请勿填写密码或无关的敏感信息。您已发起但上传失败的反馈可能暂存在本地，并在下次启动应用时重试；发生崩溃本身不会授权一次新的反馈上传。

网络信息与服务商

NiYien 通过 HTTPS 获取镜头数据和功能所需资源。Android 可检查并下载 APK 更新，由 Android 系统安装器完成安装；iOS 应用通过 App Store 更新。托管服务商会接收完成请求所需的 IP 地址和技术请求信息。服务器可能根据 IP 推算大致国家或城市，以选择服务区域；不会为此调用 GPS 或设备定位权限。依据服务器配置，由 Vercel 或 IPinfo 提供 IP 地理信息，推算结果可能被缓存。反馈限流会短期保留请求 IP；提交后的反馈记录包含 IP 哈希和服务区域，用于支持和防止滥用。

用途、存储与共享

NiYien 使用反馈排查故障，并在您提供联系方式时按需回复。Vercel 托管服务，Upstash 存储反馈记录及限流数据，Cloudflare R2 或 123 云存储按服务区域保存诊断附件。下载资源也可能由 GitHub 及上述托管服务商提供。数据可能在服务商运营的国家或地区处理。反馈和诊断资料不出售、不用于广告，访问限于支持及服务运行需要，依法必须披露的情况除外。

保留与您的选择

反馈记录和附件在调查、跟进所报问题、处理相关故障或履行适用法律义务所需期间保留。目前已确认的反馈没有按固定天数自动删除的机制。您可通过 support@niyien.com 请求查阅、更正或删除反馈；如有反馈编号请一并提供，也可提供提交日期和当时填写的邮箱。我们可能请您补充核实报告归属所必需的信息。卸载应用不会删除已提交的反馈。本地源文件和导出结果仍由您在所选位置管理。文件访问、Android 广泛存储权限、安装应用权限和系统备份设置均可在设备设置中调整，相关功能可能因此不可用。

政策更新

上方日期标明本次政策版本。数据处理方式发生实质变化时，将更新应用内及 NiYien 网站上的政策。支持邮箱：support@niyien.com；网站：https://www.niyien.com。

NiYien — Mobile Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026

Scope and contact

This policy covers NiYien for iOS and Android, identified as com.niyien.stabilizer. NiYien operates the app and its support services. Contact support@niyien.com for support, privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests. You can contact us without installing the app or sending diagnostic attachments.

Local video processing

Videos and gyroscope files you select are processed on your device to import, preview, stabilize and export your work. NiYien does not send your video or audio files to its servers. Files you deliberately share or save through another provider, and system backups you enable, are handled by that provider under its own terms and your device settings.

No automatic usage analytics

The iOS and Android builds do not generate or send anonymous installation analytics identifiers, camera-usage events or download analytics. There is no switch that re-enables these events. The app does not use these data for advertising or cross-app tracking. Necessary network services and feedback described below are separate from usage analytics.

Optional feedback and diagnostics

Pressing Submit sends your message and optional email, together with available diagnostic logs, crash records, app version, submission time and technical device information such as operating system, CPU, GPU and memory. Available lens, queue and application settings are attached. These materials can contain file names, paths, camera details, editing parameters and timing or performance information. Video and audio media are not attached. Submitting is optional; you can instead email support without diagnostics. Please do not include passwords or unrelated sensitive information. A submission that you requested but that failed to upload may be saved locally and retried when the app next starts. A crash alone does not authorize a new feedback upload.

Network information and service providers

NiYien retrieves lens data and resources needed by its features over HTTPS. Android can check for and download APK updates; installation requires the Android system installer. iOS application updates use the App Store. Our hosting services receive the IP address and technical request information needed to deliver these services. The server may derive an approximate country or city from the IP address to choose a service region; this does not use GPS or device location permission. Depending on server configuration, IP geolocation is provided by Vercel or IPinfo. IP-derived location may be cached. Feedback rate limiting retains the requesting IP temporarily, and a submitted feedback record contains an IP hash and service region for support and abuse prevention.

Purpose, storage and sharing

NiYien uses feedback to investigate faults and reply when contact information is provided. Vercel hosts the service, Upstash stores feedback records and rate-limit data, and Cloudflare R2 or 123 cloud storage stores diagnostic attachments according to the service region. Downloaded resources may also be delivered through GitHub and these hosting providers. Data may be processed in countries where those providers operate. Feedback and diagnostics are not sold or used for advertising. Access is limited to support and service operation purposes, except when disclosure is legally required.

Retention and your choices

Feedback records and attachments are retained while needed to investigate and follow up on the reported problem, address related faults or meet applicable legal obligations. There is currently no automatic fixed-day deletion schedule for confirmed reports. You may ask support@niyien.com to locate, provide, correct or delete a report; include its feedback identifier if available, or the submission date and email you used. We may ask for information needed to verify that the report is yours. Removing the app does not delete feedback already submitted. Local source and exported files remain under your control in the storage locations you chose. File access, Android broad storage access, Android installation permission and system backup settings can be changed in device settings; affected features may become unavailable.

Policy updates

The date above identifies this policy revision. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in the policy available in the app and on the NiYien website. NiYien support: support@niyien.com. Website: https://www.niyien.com.
