NiYien generated review sample

niyien-demo.mp4 is a six-second, 640 x 360, 30 fps synthetic roll-motion
demonstration. The grid and geometric shapes are generated by
_scripts/generate_mobile_demo.py. The matching 400 Hz angular velocity is
in niyien-demo.gcsv; the lens model, zero time offset and export settings
are in niyien-demo.gyroflow.

The sample contains no third-party footage, people, audio or private camera
metadata. It demonstrates the stabilization workflow, not measured camera
quality, hardware support or iOS performance.
