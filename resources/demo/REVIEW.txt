NiYien review sample / NiYien 审核示例

No account, camera or external hardware is required.

Recommended iOS review workflow:
1. Open Settings → App → Help and support.
2. Tap Try a generated demo. The app returns to the video list and imports
   the bundled video, matching motion data and synthetic lens model.
3. Open the sample's preview and compare Original / Stabilized.
4. Return to the video list and tap Export stabilized video. Keep the app
   in the foreground until processing finishes.
5. Open the completed task and tap Share / Save to Files to retrieve the
   MP4. The expected output is 640 x 360, 30 fps and approximately 6 seconds.

The Stabilize (for plugins) action exports a .gyroflow project for desktop
plugins; use Export stabilized video to produce a playable video.

Alternative for desktop testing:
Extract this archive, keep its files together and open niyien-demo.gyroflow
in NiYien. The project references its video and motion log by filename.
On iOS, the built-in entry avoids granting access to separate sibling files.

中文操作：
1. 打开“设置 → 应用 → 帮助与支持”，点击“体验内置示例”。
2. 导入完成后打开视频，比较“原始／稳定后”。
3. 返回列表，点击“导出稳定视频”，处理期间保持应用在前台。
4. 打开完成的任务，点击“分享／存储到文件”，取回 MP4。

本示例是自行生成的几何图案、模拟横滚和配套运动数据，无人物、
音频或第三方相机素材。它用于演示流程，不代表真实相机画质、
支持范围或 iOS 性能。普通手机视频可能没有陀螺仪稳定所需的数据。
